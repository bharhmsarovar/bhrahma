Smart Booking

Why You Should Compare Rooms, Not Just Prices

When looking for an AC room around a ₹2,500 budget in Pushkar, the headline price is only one part of what you are paying for. Two rooms listed at a similar rate can be very different in what they offer.

Bed type and size

Check whether the room has a single bed, a double bed or a king bed. A king bed gives more space for two people and affects the comfort of your stay considerably.

Bathroom — attached or shared

A shared bathroom means you may be using the same facility as other rooms. A private attached bathroom is a meaningful upgrade in comfort, especially for longer stays.

Air conditioning — check it works

Pushkar summers are very hot and winters can be cold at night. Confirm that the AC unit is functional and able to cool or heat the room. Ask what type of AC is in the room.

Free Wi-Fi in the room

Some properties charge for Wi-Fi or provide it only in common areas. Confirm that Wi-Fi is available inside your room and ask about the typical speed or reliability.

Total price including GST

Room rates in India are usually shown excluding GST. Ask for the total price including all taxes for your selected dates before you finalise anything.

Views and balcony

A balcony room with mountain and city views adds to the experience of staying in Pushkar, particularly in the morning. It is worth asking whether this is available at your budget.

Before You Book

What to Confirm Before You Finalise a Room

These are the questions worth asking any property — including us — before you confirm your stay in Pushkar.

  • Is the room available for your exact dates? Availability changes constantly. Always confirm the specific room for your check-in and check-out dates before making any payment.
  • What is the total price including GST? Ask for the final amount you will pay, including all applicable taxes. A rate that looks affordable can increase once GST is added.
  • Does the rate include breakfast? At Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe, breakfast is optional and can be added to your stay. Ask about the cost and menu when you enquire.
  • How many guests does the rate cover? A rate quoted per room may cover only one or two adults. If you are bringing a child or need an extra bed, confirm the total cost for your full group.
  • What is the cancellation policy? Ask what happens if your plans change. Confirm this in writing before you pay or confirm a booking, especially during festival periods when demand is high.
  • Is parking available if you are arriving by car? Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe offers free on-site parking. If you are arriving in a large vehicle, confirm access with us in advance as Choti Basti lanes are narrow.
  • Is the rate higher during Pushkar Fair or Kartik Purnima? Accommodation rates in Pushkar often go up significantly during festival periods. If you are visiting around these dates, book early and confirm the rate for that specific period.
  • What payment methods are accepted? Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe accepts card, UPI and cash. Confirm the preferred payment method and timing before arrival.
