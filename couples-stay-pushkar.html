Choosing a room for two

All five rooms at Brahma Sarovar have a king bed and air conditioning, which makes any of them a workable option for two adults. The differences between rooms come down to bathroom arrangement, whether the room has a private balcony, and the view.

Deluxe Double Rooms (two available)

These are the standard double rooms. Both have a king bed, air conditioning, a flat-screen TV and free Wi-Fi. One has a shared bathroom and the other a private attached bathroom. If a private bathroom matters to you, confirm which room you are booking when you enquire.

Deluxe Double Room with Balcony

This room adds a private balcony with mountain and city views. It is listed for two adults and also offered for single occupancy. Confirm the exact booking arrangement in your enquiry.

Double Room with Extra Bed

This room has a king bed plus a futon, suitable for up to three adults. Two people travelling together who want more floor space might consider this option. Ask about the futon arrangement when you enquire.

Before you arrive

Identification, check-in and policies

Indian law requires accommodation properties to record guest identification at check-in. Both guests should carry valid government-issued photo identification. Acceptable documents typically include an Aadhaar card, voter ID, driving licence or passport, but the specific documents a property accepts can vary.

Contact Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe directly to confirm which forms of identification are accepted before your visit, so that you are not turned away at arrival.

Check-in time

Check-in is available 24 hours. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. If you plan to arrive late at night or very early in the morning, mention this in your enquiry so the property can plan accordingly.

Policies and unmarried couples

Accommodation policies vary between properties and across states in India. This guide does not make any claim about the policy at Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe regarding unmarried couples. Contact the property on WhatsApp before booking to ask any questions about their policy and to confirm the room is available on your dates.
