Before You Book

Questions Families Should Confirm

Not all policies are published on the website. Confirm these points before you finalise your booking.

Child Age Limit

The Deluxe Double Room with Balcony (2 Adults + 1 Child) is listed for one child, but the eligible age range has not been confirmed on the website. Include your child's age in your WhatsApp message and ask the property to confirm eligibility before you book.

Extra-Bed Availability

An extra bed is available on request at Rs 300 per adult per night plus GST. Ask which rooms can accommodate the extra bed and whether there is a limit on the number of extra beds per room.

Maximum Guests Per Room

The Deluxe Double Rooms each have a maximum of 2 adults. The balcony family room adds 1 child on top of 2 adults. The Double Room with Extra Bed is listed for up to 3 adults. Do not assume every room can accommodate your full group — confirm in advance.

Breakfast for Children

Breakfast is optional and can be added to your stay. The menu and pricing have not been published on the website. Ask about the current breakfast cost, what is offered and whether children's portions are available.

Check-In with Children

Check-in is available 24 hours. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. If you are arriving late at night with children, tell the property your estimated arrival time in advance so they can be ready to receive you.

Parking

Free parking is available on site. If you are driving with car seats or larger family luggage, confirm the parking arrangement when you enquire so nothing is a surprise on arrival.

Payment Options

The property accepts card, UPI and cash. All rates are plus GST. The current rate for any room is not published on the website — ask on WhatsApp before you book.

Fair and Festival Periods

Pushkar becomes very busy during the Pushkar Fair and Kartik Purnima in November. Book early if you are visiting at that time. Confirm your room well in advance and keep a copy of every reply confirming your room and dates.

Your Pre-Booking Checklist for Families

  • Confirm your child's age is eligible for the family room.
  • State the total number of guests — adults and children — in your enquiry.
  • Ask about extra-bed availability and cost if your group needs more beds.
  • Ask whether breakfast is available for children and what it costs.
  • Confirm the child age limit applies to the room you choose.
  • Confirm check-in time if you are arriving late or very early.
  • Ask about parking if you are coming by car.
  • Wait for a written reply confirming your room and dates before you travel.
