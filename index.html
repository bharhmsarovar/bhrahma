Questions guests ask

How many rooms does Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe have?

We have five rooms with six beds: Deluxe Double Room, Deluxe Double Room with Balcony, Deluxe Double Room with Balcony (2 Adults + 1 Child) and Double Room with Extra Bed. Open any room on this site to see its bed, facilities and photos.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is available 24 hours. Check-out is at 11:00 AM.

Can children stay, and is it good for families?

Yes. 2 children can stay, so the home stay is a good fit for families.

Can I get an extra bed?

An extra bed is available on request at Rs 300 per adult. Rates are plus GST.

How can I pay?

You can pay by card, UPI or cash.

Which rooms have a balcony and a view?

The Deluxe Double Room with Balcony and the Deluxe Double Room with Balcony (2 Adults + 1 Child) both have a balcony with mountain and city views. They also have a king bed, air conditioning, a private attached bathroom, a flat-screen TV and free Wi-Fi.

Do the rooms have air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

The Deluxe Double Room, the Deluxe Double Room, the Deluxe Double Room with Balcony, the Deluxe Double Room with Balcony (2 Adults + 1 Child) and the Double Room with Extra Bed are all listed with air conditioning, a flat-screen TV and free Wi-Fi. Open a room page for its full details.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is optional and can be added to your stay. Ask us on WhatsApp for the price, timing and menu.

Pushkar Fair 2026: Dates and How to Plan Your Stay

Fair dates depend on the lunar calendar and the official programme can change. Use this page to plan, then check official updates before you travel.

Last updated: 28 Oct 2026

Dates

Status: dates are reported, and the programme can change.

  • Fair dates: Rajasthan Tourism lists the Pushkar Fair 2026 for 17 to 24 November 2026.
  • Holy days (calendar-based): Drik Panchang lists Devutthana Ekadashi on Friday 20 November and Kartik Purnima with Pushkara Snana on Tuesday 24 November 2026. These are calendar dates, not a fair announcement.
  • Programme (reported): The district administration released a day-wise programme on Wednesday 7 October 2026, as reported by ETV Bharat and Sabguru News. We have not opened the official document. It reports that tourism events open on 17 November at 10 am at the Mela Ground, traditional games are on 18 November, and the closing programme is on 24 November at 6 pm. Animal trading is reported to start informally around 10 November.

Check official updates before you travel. The programme can change. Do not book travel on the dates above without confirming them.
