Things to Do

What are the best things to do in Pushkar?

Our Things to Do in Pushkar guide rounds up the lake, ghats, temples, the bazaar and a camel safari into the dunes.

Is Pushkar Lake sacred?

Yes — Pushkar Lake is considered one of Hinduism's sacred lakes, with ghats used for ritual bathing. See our Pushkar Lake Guide for customs around it.

Where can I go for a camel safari near Pushkar?

The dunes just outside town are the usual starting point — see our Camel Safari and Sunset Dunes guide before booking.

How many days are enough to see Pushkar?

Temples

Which temples should I visit in Pushkar besides the Brahma Temple?

The Varaha Temple, Rangji Temple and Savitri Temple are the next most visited, each with its own history and architecture.

What is special about the Brahma Temple?

It's widely cited as one of the very few temples in India dedicated to Brahma, which is part of why Pushkar draws pilgrims year-round.

How do I reach Savitri Temple?

It sits on a hilltop overlooking Pushkar, reachable by a cable car or a climb on foot — see our Savitri Temple guide.

What should I wear or avoid when visiting Pushkar's temples?

Modest, covering clothing is expected, and leather items are best left outside temple areas — see our Pushkar Etiquette and Safety guide for details.

Pushkar Fair

What is the Pushkar Fair?

It's Pushkar's best-known annual event — part livestock fair, part cultural festival — drawing traders, pilgrims and travellers from across the region. See our Pushkar Fair Guide.

When does the Pushkar Fair usually happen?

It falls around the Hindu month of Kartik, typically October or November — see our Pushkar Fair Guide and Best Time to Visit guide for how to plan around it.

What is Kartik Purnima and why do people bathe in Pushkar Lake?

Kartik Purnima is a full-moon ritual bathing day considered especially auspicious at Pushkar Lake — see our Kartik Purnima and Pushkar Snan guide.

What is the best time of year to visit Pushkar?

Our Best Time to Visit Pushkar guide covers this season by season, including fair season, the busiest and most atmospheric time to be here.

Essentials

What should first-time visitors to Pushkar know?

See our guide for first-time visitors — it covers orientation, what to expect, and practical basics before you arrive.

Is Pushkar vegetarian and alcohol-free?

Pushkar town is widely reported to restrict meat and alcohol sales out of respect for its sacred status — see our Pushkar Etiquette and Safety guide, and confirm specifics locally if it matters for your plans.

Can I visit Ajmer Sharif from Pushkar in a day?

Yes — the Dargah is a short drive away and commonly done as a half-day or full-day trip. See our Ajmer Sharif Day Trip guide.

Is Pushkar safe for solo and family travellers?

Pushkar is a well-established travel destination with steady tourist footfall — see our Etiquette and Safety guide and our Solo Traveller Stay guide for specifics.
