Pushkar Lake

About Pushkar Lake and Its Ghats

Pushkar Lake is a sacred lake at the heart of Pushkar town in Rajasthan. It is surrounded by 52 ghats — stone steps that lead directly to the water — where pilgrims perform ritual bathing, prayers and ceremonies throughout the year.

The lake is particularly significant during Kartik Purnima, when large numbers of pilgrims arrive for Pushkar Snan, a holy dip believed to wash away sins. The Pushkar Fair, held annually in the Kartik month, is one of the most well-known events in Rajasthan and draws visitors from across the country and abroad.

The ghats are open throughout the day. Visitors are asked to dress modestly, remove footwear near the ghat steps, and avoid carrying leather items to the lake. Loud behaviour and photography of bathing pilgrims is generally unwelcome — follow any instructions you see on site.

🕌

52 Ghats Around the Lake

Each ghat has its own name and association. Mahadeva Ghat is one of the main ghats and is approximately 400 m from our stay in Choti Basti. Pushkar Ghat is approximately 800 m away.

🛕

Brahma Temple Nearby

One of very few temples in India dedicated to Lord Brahma, the Brahma Temple is approximately 1 km from our property. Visitors are asked to remove footwear before entering.

🌅

Best Time to Visit the Ghats

Early morning is the quietest time. Sunset is popular but busier. During the Pushkar Fair and Kartik Purnima, ghats are much more crowded and the area becomes lively day and night.

👗

What to Wear and Carry

Dress modestly — covered shoulders and knees are appropriate near the lake. Avoid leather items near the ghats. Bring water if you plan to walk around multiple ghats.

The Neighbourhood

What Is Choti Basti?

Choti Basti is a residential area of Pushkar town, situated close to the lake and the main ghat area. It is a quieter part of town compared to the main market street, while still being within walking distance of Pushkar's key sights.

The area is well-suited for visitors who want a peaceful place to return to after exploring the lake, temples and bazaars. Streets in Choti Basti are narrow in parts, so large vehicles may not reach every address. Auto-rickshaws and e-rickshaws are the most practical way to arrive with luggage.

There are small shops and eateries nearby. The main Pushkar bazaar, which runs along the lake, is a short walk away.

How far is Pushkar Lake from your home stay?

Mahadeva Ghat on Pushkar Lake is approximately 400 m from our property in Choti Basti. Pushkar Ghat is approximately 800 m. These are estimates — the actual walking route along the lanes may be slightly longer. We recommend confirming the route on Google Maps before you visit.

Choti Basti is a residential area close to the lake and ghats, while being quieter than the main market area. It is walkable to most of Pushkar's main sights. Narrow streets mean auto-rickshaws are the most practical way to arrive with luggage.
