Overview

Choosing accommodation in Pushkar

Pushkar is a compact pilgrimage town in Rajasthan. Most of its accommodation is within walking distance of the lake and the main temples, but the experience — noise levels, proximity to the ghats, room quality, facilities and price — varies considerably from one property to the next.

This guide covers the practical questions to ask when choosing where to stay: location, walking needs, room type, rooftop access, family requirements, budget and travel dates. It also explains what to confirm in writing before finalising any booking.

Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe is one of the options in Pushkar. It is a home stay with five rooms and a rooftop cafe in the Choti Basti area. Details below are based on verified facts from the property.

Decision guide

What to consider before you book

Run through each of these before shortlisting accommodation in Pushkar. Small differences between properties matter more when you are spending several days on foot.

📍

Location and neighbourhood

Pushkar has several distinct areas — the main bazaar, the ghats, Choti Basti, Badi Basti and roads further from the lake. How close you are to the lake, the Brahma Temple and the main market will affect how much you walk each day and the noise level at night.

  • Ask for the property's street address and locality, not just "near Pushkar Lake"
  • Check the pin on Google Maps before booking
  • Ask about the walking route to the ghats — not just the straight-line distance
🚶

Walking and transport needs

Most visitors explore Pushkar on foot. Auto-rickshaws and e-rickshaws are available, but lanes near the lake can be narrow. If someone in your group has difficulty walking, ask specifically about the ground-floor rooms and the route from the property to the main road.

  • Ask about the road surface and whether the route is paved
  • Confirm whether parking is available if you are travelling by car
  • Ask about the nearest pickup point for taxis and auto-rickshaws
🛏

Room type and bed arrangement

Room categories in Pushkar cover a wide range. A "double room" at one property may mean a single small bed; at another, a king bed. Balcony rooms, views and attached bathrooms are not standard across all properties. Verify the actual bed type, size and bathroom arrangement before you book.

  • Ask for the exact bed type — king, queen, twin or single
  • Confirm whether the bathroom is attached or shared
  • Ask specifically if you need a balcony or a view
❄️

Air conditioning and Wi-Fi

In Rajasthan, particularly from April to June, air conditioning makes a material difference to comfort. Not all rooms in Pushkar have it. Wi-Fi quality also varies. Ask whether AC is included in the quoted rate or charged separately.

  • Confirm AC is available in the specific room, not just at the property
  • Ask whether the AC rate is included or extra
  • Ask whether Wi-Fi is free and available in the room
☕

Rooftop cafe access

A rooftop with views over Pushkar's hills and town is a significant draw. Some properties have a working cafe; others have a terrace without food or drink service. Confirm whether the cafe is open, what it serves, whether breakfast is included in your rate, and whether the rooftop is available throughout the day.

  • Ask whether the cafe serves breakfast and what the timing is
  • Confirm whether the rooftop is accessible to all guests
  • Ask if the cafe is open to visitors who are not staying at the property
👨‍👩‍👧

Family and small-group requirements

If you are travelling with children or a group of three adults, the available room types narrow quickly. Check the specific occupancy policy for each room — not all rooms that accept children are configured the same way. Ask about extra-bed arrangements and the age limit for children.

  • Ask whether children are permitted and the age limit that applies
  • Ask for the occupancy limit per room
  • Confirm the extra-bed arrangement and its cost in advance
💰

Budget and total price

The headline nightly rate is not always the full cost. In India, GST applies on top of the room rate. Extra beds, breakfast and early check-in may be charged separately. Ask for the complete price for your dates, including all applicable taxes, before confirming a booking.

  • Ask for the all-inclusive total for your specific dates and guests
  • Confirm whether GST is included in the quoted price
  • Ask whether breakfast is included or charged separately
📅

Travel dates and advance booking

Pushkar is significantly busier during the Pushkar Fair (around Kartik Purnima in October–November) and other festival periods. Rooms fill well in advance during these periods. Outside peak season, same-day bookings may be possible. If your dates coincide with any major event, enquire and confirm as early as you can.

  • Check whether your dates fall near the Pushkar Fair or a religious festival
  • Book early for November and fair-season dates
  • Ask about the cancellation policy before you confirm

Before you confirm

What to confirm in writing before booking any property in Pushkar

A friendly conversation or a WhatsApp message is not a confirmed booking. Before you finalise plans for any property, get written confirmation of the following:

Checklist — confirm these before you pay or travel

  • The exact room name and its bed type, bathroom type and floor
  • The check-in and check-out times that apply to your booking
  • The total price for your dates, number of guests and room — including GST
  • Whether breakfast is included and at what time it is served
  • Whether AC is included in the rate or charged as an add-on
  • The extra-bed cost and availability, if you need one
  • The child age policy, if children are travelling with you
  • The cancellation and refund policy, in writing
  • The payment method accepted — cash, card, UPI or online payment
  • The property's exact address and how to reach it from the bus stand or your arrival point

These questions apply to any property in Pushkar. If a property is unable or unwilling to confirm any of the above before you arrive, factor that into your decision.

Questions about where to stay in Pushkar

Is Choti Basti a good area to stay in Pushkar?

Choti Basti is a residential locality close to Pushkar Lake and several ghats and temples. Brahma Sarovar Home Stay and Cafe is in this area, on Heloz Road. Whether it suits you depends on what you plan to do. If walking to the lake each day is a priority, the area's proximity to the ghats is a practical advantage. Check the exact walking route on Google Maps before booking.

How many rooms does Brahma Sarovar have and what types are available?

The property has five rooms: two Deluxe Double Rooms (one with a shared bathroom, one with a private attached bathroom), a Deluxe Double Room with Balcony, a Deluxe Double Room with Balcony for 2 Adults + 1 Child, and a Double Room with Extra Bed for up to three adults. All rooms have air conditioning, a flat-screen TV and free Wi-Fi. See all rooms in detail.

Which rooms have a balcony and views?

Two rooms have a balcony with mountain and city views: the Deluxe Double Room with Balcony and the Deluxe Double Room with Balcony (2 Adults + 1 Child). Both have air conditioning and private attached bathrooms.

What is the check-in and check-out policy?

Check-in is available 24 hours. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. This means there is no restricted check-in window — useful if you are arriving on a late night bus or train.

How do I enquire about current room rates?

Rates are not published as fixed prices on the website. Use the WhatsApp enquiry button or the Booking.com listing to request current rates for your dates, room type and number of guests. Always confirm the total price including GST before finalising.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is not automatically included — it is optional and can be added to your stay. Ask about the price, timing and menu when you enquire.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available on site and does not need to be reserved in advance. Mention your vehicle in your WhatsApp enquiry if you want to confirm this for your visit.

When should I book in advance?

If your dates fall during the Pushkar Fair (around Kartik Purnima in October–November), book as early as possible. Demand across Pushkar is high during this period. For other times of year, enquire with your travel dates to check availability.

How do I reach Brahma Sarovar from Ajmer?

Ajmer Junction railway station is approximately 13 km from the property. Taxis, shared cabs and buses run between Ajmer and Pushkar. Once in Pushkar, use the Google Maps link below to navigate to Heloz Road in Choti Basti, or call the property on +91 87666 22622 for directions.
